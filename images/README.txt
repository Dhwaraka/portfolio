Drop your photos in this folder.

Recommended filenames: lowercase, dashes instead of spaces.
  ✅ dispenser-1.jpg
  ✅ reformer-installed.jpg
  ❌ IMG_4523 (1).JPG

Recommended formats: .jpg or .webp (smaller files, faster site).
Recommended max size per image: under 500 KB.

Use squoosh.app (free, runs in your browser) to shrink photos
before uploading them here.

You can delete this README.txt file once you've added your photos.
